---
name: Pricing and Billing Policy
description: Founder discount threshold, treatment of existing agreements, and preferred payment methods.
---

The intended launch offer is R$500/month until the business reaches 100 customers; after that, new contracts use R$890/month. Existing customers keep the price in their signed agreement. The preferred future billing model is automatic monthly Mercado Pago recurring payments, with manual Pix available as an alternative.

**Why:** the user wants a temporary discount while building a sustainable base of 100 customers and explicitly chose to preserve existing customer terms.

**How to apply:** do not change existing customers' charges without their acceptance. Define how the 100-customer threshold is counted before implementing automatic price changes. The current payment flow is one-time Pix; do not describe automatic recurrence as available until it is implemented and verified.
